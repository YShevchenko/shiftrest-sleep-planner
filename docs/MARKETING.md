# Shift Worker Sleep Marketing Strategy

**Document:** MARKETING.md  
**Product:** Shift Worker Sleep  

## 1. Viability Classification
**Tier 2: Smart Utility**  
Highly specific niche targeting allows for very cheap Cost Per Install (CPI).

## 2. Target Audience & Ad Platforms
- **Primary Platform:** Facebook & LinkedIn Ads.
- **Targeting:** Exact Job Titles ONLY. "Registered Nurse", "EMT", "Police Officer", "Warehouse Manager", "Pilot". Do not target broad "sleep" interests.

## 3. Core Marketing Hook (Ad Copy)
"Generic sleep apps assume you go to bed at 10 PM. This app actually does the sleep math for your rotating shifts and quick turnarounds. Enter your shift, get a sleep window."

## 4. Geographic & Pricing Strategy
- **Global:** $4.99 one-time purchase (matches SPEC.md). Price point covers paid acquisition costs in most regions. Niche targeting (shift workers, nurses, factory workers) keeps cost per install low.
- **Expansion:** Shift work is universal. Translation to Spanish and French opens up massive markets where shift work is highly prevalent and ad clicks are cheap.
