🌙 **Sleep smarter between your shifts.**

Shift work wrecks your sleep. Whether you're on nights, rotating shifts, or 12-hour blocks, your body's clock fights you every time. ShiftRest is the only sleep planner built specifically for shift workers — it calculates exactly when to sleep, how long to sleep, and how to get the most recovery out of every gap between shifts.

### 🧠 Powered by the Borbely Two-Process Circadian Model

ShiftRest doesn't guess — it applies the same science used in clinical sleep research. The Borbely two-process model combines your sleep pressure (Process S) with your body's natural circadian rhythm (Process C) to identify exactly when your sleep will be most efficient. Sleep at the right time and wake up feeling rested. Sleep at the wrong time and you waste hours lying awake.

### 📅 Shift Schedule Tracking

Enter your upcoming shifts once. ShiftRest automatically:
- Calculates the gap between every consecutive shift
- Determines the best sleep strategy for each gap (single block, core + nap, or split sleep)
- Tracks your running sleep debt so you know how much recovery you need on your days off

---

### 💤 Smart Sleep Window Calculator

Three strategies, automatically chosen based on your time window:
- **Single block (7+ hour gap):** One full 7–8 hour sleep block, timed for maximum circadian efficiency
- **Core + nap (4–7 hour gap):** A core sleep block plus a strategic afternoon nap to hit your sleep debt
- **Split sleep (under 4 hours):** Two smaller blocks timed for the deepest circadian troughs — the best you can do in a tight turnaround

Waking mid-cycle leaves you groggy for hours. ShiftRest's smart alarm finds the closest end of a 90-minute sleep cycle within your allowable wake window — so you always rise at the lightest sleep stage, ready to work.
