## Description (max 4000 chars)

🌙 Sleep smarter between your shifts.

Shift work wrecks your sleep. Whether you're on nights, rotating shifts, or 12-hour blocks, your body's clock fights you every time. ShiftRest is the only sleep planner built specifically for shift workers — it calculates exactly when to sleep, how long to sleep, and how to get the most recovery out of every gap between shifts.

---

POWERED BY SCIENCE

ShiftRest applies the Borbely two-process circadian model — the same framework used in clinical sleep research. It combines your sleep pressure (Process S) with your natural circadian rhythm (Process C) to find exactly when your sleep will be deepest and most restorative. Sleep at the right time, wake up ready to work.

---

SHIFT SCHEDULE TRACKING

Enter your upcoming shifts once. ShiftRest automatically calculates the gap between every consecutive shift and recommends the best sleep strategy for each one. It also tracks your running sleep debt so you know how much recovery to prioritize on your days off.

---

SMART SLEEP WINDOW CALCULATOR

Three evidence-based strategies, chosen automatically for your time window:

• Single block — For 7+ hour gaps. One optimally timed full sleep block.
• Core + nap — For 4–7 hour gaps. Core sleep plus a strategic afternoon nap.
• Split sleep — For under 4 hours. Two blocks timed for circadian troughs.

---

90-MINUTE CYCLE ALARM

Waking mid-cycle leaves you groggy for hours. ShiftRest's smart alarm finds the closest end of a 90-minute sleep cycle within your wake window — so you always rise at the lightest sleep stage, feeling as rested as possible.

---

DARK ROOM MODE

One tap turns your screen pitch black (with a dim red-light option for night navigation). Keeps your screen active so you're not fumbling with your phone when you need it least.

---

WHITE NOISE & SLEEP SOUNDS

Built-in noise player with locally generated sounds — no internet required:
• White noise, pink noise, brown noise
• Rain, ocean waves, fan

---

CIRCADIAN RHYTHM VISUALIZER

See your full 24-hour alertness curve. Identify your circadian trough (best time to sleep) and your peak alertness window. Understand exactly why falling asleep at certain times feels impossible.
